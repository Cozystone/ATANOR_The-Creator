## Controlled dims (the being's own measure, no names)
For each feeling dim: the share of its one-beat change explained by which combo was done (eta^2), compared with the same share after shuffling the
combo labels (200 shuffles). A dim is "controlled" if its eta^2 exceeds the 99th shuffle percentile AND is at least .1. In the room this should keep the
felt motion dims (legs .20, neck .37) - checked, not tuned.
Effects e(a) = mean one-beat change of the controlled dims for combo a; kinship as D9. Episodic score as D8/D9 with this kinship.

## Ruler (ecological)
At the fork start each combo is held 3 beats (the being's own typical hold), then the being continues with its own lived value until beat 150;
two clones per combo and need; truth = needed-channel recovery; its split-half reliability is reported.
