# P1: an innate path-integration organ (registered 2026-11-24, before any run)

Founder, 2026-11-24, asked whether to give the being path integration (knowing where it is from its own motion) as an innate organ: "타고난 기관으로".

## Why
D25 - D32: what the being lacks is knowing where it is. A stitched map read on true places gives the way at .81 - 1.00 per cell (D26); read on the
being's own learned place code it fails unless the experience already holds trips (D30), and the being makes none (E1, E1b, N1 so far). The earlier
maze successes (room3d home world) had place given by hand: exact step sense, a built grid map, a 'go to the unknown' rule, eye readouts of direction.
Animals are born with place and grid circuits (O'Keefe & Nadel 1978; Hafting et al. 2005; rat pups have head-direction and grid-like cells on their
first outings, Wills et al. 2010, Langston et al. 2010). Path integration by the own motion is the oldest of them (desert ant, Wehner; Mittelstaedt).
The roadmap approved on 2026-11-21 said: if learned place fails at this scale, keep the spatial organ innate and say so. This is that step.

## The organ (fixed, not learned; in the being, not the world)
Input: the body's own felt motion (the room body already gives it: forward and sideways step in the frame it faced, and the turn). The organ sums it from
the moment the life began: heading h and place (px, py) relative to the birth place and birth heading. Output, read as a grid-cell-like code:
for 3 directions 60 degrees apart and 4 scales (2, 4, 8, 16 cells), cos and sin of 2 pi (place . direction) / scale (24 numbers), plus cos h, sin h.
Nothing else is innate: no grid map, no 'go to the unknown', no rule about where to go. The map and the going stay learned on top of this code.
Said plainly: in the room the felt motion is exact, so this organ is the truth here; a real body drifts. Drift, and correcting it by the eye, is later work.
The body contract gets one flag, Channel(motion=True), telling the being which signal is its own motion (as the bedrock flag tells it which is its lack).

# D33 (registered before running): can the being learn to go to a remembered place from its own play?
Founder's note of 2026-11-21 (evening), not used until now: "motor babbling in a protected childhood (stores not draining) before goals". And the
reaching literature: infants learn to reach by babbling and then by practising toward things (von Hofsten; Thelen), robots by goal babbling
(Rolf, Steil & Gienger 2010) and by relabelling their own attempts (GCSL, Ghosh et al. 2019). D32 / D32b failed with a judge-driven traveller's
gestures and with learned place units; here the gestures are the being's own and the place is the innate code.
Probe (judge-only, offline, not yet a life): 64 bodies per layout, stores not draining.
- play 0: 3,000 beats of uniform random gestures from where life starts (babbling);
- skill: one network (outside senses + organ code (place and heading) + goal place code) -> gesture, trained by hindsight on the body's own play:
  the goal is the place code 1 - 64 beats later in the same play; 6,000 updates;
- play 1 and 2 (goal practice): 3,000 beats each in which every body tries to reach random places it has been, with the skill (10% random gestures,
  new goal on arrival or after 200 beats); each round retrains on all play so far.
Test as D31: bodies with one inside low at the junction, the wrong spring and the bottom of the stem; the goal is the place code of a moment of
relief of that inside in the bodies' own play (one per body); 400 beats. Controls: the other inside's relief place; no goal.
Reading (fixed now): recovery >= .6 from the wrong spring and from the stem in 4 of 6 with the wrong-goal control at most half -> the going can be learned
from own play with the innate place sense; then the life build is: childhood play (protected), relief remembered with its place, and the held goal
(persisting pressure toward the remembered place of what is lacking) feeding this skill - registered then. Not met -> report with the numbers.
D33 amendment (before any goal-practice result, said plainly): round 0 (babbling only) gave loss 4.50 of 4.50 and 0 recovery everywhere, controls
included - the skill learned from random babbling is near uniform, and following it by argmax picks one fixed gesture (bodies stand still). I stopped
the run there. As in GCSL (Ghosh et al. 2019), the skill is now followed by sampling its own distribution, in practice and in the test alike; goal
horizon 1 - 32 beats; 5 rounds of practice instead of 2. Bars unchanged.
D33 second fix (my bug, before any valid result): the skill's input was scaled by each sense's spread + .001; senses that barely vary in babbling
became huge (first loss 19.5) and the network collapsed to uniform - that, not the data, was the 4.50 of both earlier starts. Inputs are now scaled
with a spread of at least .05 and clipped at 5; the one-beat check then learns (4.50 -> 3.11 in 1,500 updates). The runs before this fix are void.

## D33c diagnostic (9101): why nothing arrives from the wrong spring - my design error in the organ's readout
Bodies at the wrong spring sat in a map unit that also held the needed spring's relief (unit 14 for water while standing at food); the map's chain
said 'one step away', the goal never pointed across the room, and the bodies moved at most 2.1 cells. Cause: the grid code had four scales of equal
strength, so code distance was ruled by the finest scale - one cell apart 3.45, the two springs (9 cells apart) 3.01. Anything that compares codes
(k-means units, nearest unit) mixed far places. Fixed in the organ (PathOrgan): each scale as strong as its length, plus a 32-cell scale (32 numbers);
code distance now follows true distance (Spearman .998 over a 16 x 8 field; 1 cell 2.55, 9 cells 13.1, 15 cells 15.9).
This is a fix of the innate organ's readout, not of behaviour; P1a, P1b, D33 and D33b ran with the old readout. D33b is rerun as registered, same bars.

## D33c, second look (9101, 9102, fixed readout): my second bug - the map linked every place to the birth place
From the wrong spring the map's chain was two steps: spring -> birth place (bottom of the stem) -> the needed spring, and the bodies, given the far
birth place as their next goal, moved at most 2.1 cells. The link came from my map building: all plays were stacked in time and the last beat of each
play was counted as a step to the first beat of the next (the birth place). With so few links needed for 'neighbour', every place neighboured birth.
(The organ itself checks out: integrated place vs place given to judge-placed bodies differ by 2e-6.) Fixed: transitions are counted inside each play
only. D33b is rerun as registered, same bars.
