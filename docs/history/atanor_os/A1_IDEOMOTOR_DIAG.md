# D9: actions known by their felt effects (registered 2026-11-24, before running)

D8 + count: about 59 lived moments resemble the fork moment, and only 19-37 of the 90 combos were ever done there (about 2 times each).
No score that knows a combo only by its index can rank 90 combos from that. Theory (ideomotor; James 1890, Hommel 2001): an animal knows its actions
by their felt effects, so what was learned for one movement holds for every action that moves the body alike. How a combo moves the body is the
same everywhere, so its effect is learned from all 12,000 lived beats (~130 per combo).

Effect vector e(a): the mean one-beat change of the non-bedrock feelings (x2 - x) over every lived beat with combo a, standardised per dim over combos.
Combo kinship k(a, b) = exp(-|e(a) - e(b)|^2 / median over pairs). Episodic score (as D8, same similarity w_t to the lived moments, same recalled
bedrock outcome G_t applied to the current stores): Score(a) = sum_t w_t k(a, a_t) G_t / (sum_t w_t k(a, a_t) + 1). Nothing is tuned on the truth;
the kinship is the being's own, learned from its life. Same truth and metrics as D2-D8.
