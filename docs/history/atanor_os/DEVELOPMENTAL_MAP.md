# Developmental map: newborn scaffolds and where ATANOR stands (2026-11-25)

Purpose (founder, 2026-11-25): a map of where the being is, not a shopping list. For each scaffold that human (and animal) newborns bring,
what the evidence says it is (an innate answer, or a bias that experience completes), and what the current being has - measured, not hoped.
Survey with sources and contested marks: docs_tmp/infant_scaffolds_survey.md (literature; items marked CONTESTED / UNSURE there).
Status: ✅ has it (measured) · ◐ part of it / being tested · ✗ absent · ? not measured on the main line.
"Older version" = tried in an earlier ATANOR (e.g. the ATANOR DEMO packages); history, not present ability.

## What the evidence says overall
Most newborn scaffolds are attention / learning biases that experience completes (predispositions + learning: Versace, Vallortigara et al.
2018; Johnson & Morton CONSPEC/CONLERN). Few are answers: head direction and boundary use in rat pups before eye opening, and object permanence
in newly hatched chicks (the clearest). Several famous infant "abilities" do not hold as innate benchmarks: neonatal imitation (Oostenbroek
2016; Davis 2021 meta-analysis split by lab), infant false belief (ManyBabies 2), helper-over-hinderer preference (ManyBabies 4, 2025). So the
being does not need innate answers in those places - it needs the biases that let learning start.

## Where it stands, in one line
A young animal with an innate sense of where it is and which way it faces, a protected babbling childhood, a remembered map of where its
needs were met, a first channel for feeling another's relief - and no notion yet that some things in the world move by themselves, react to
it, or want something.

## The gap the founder named first: agent detection
Minimal biases the literature points to, for a being with an eye, felt own motion and inside levels (survey section 3; not designs yet):
1. attention to seen motion its own motion does not explain - onset and abrupt speed change (needs its own forecast of the frame from its
   act; the imagination organ / fovea surprise are near this);
2. a contingency sorting of what it sees: perfectly predicted = own body, imperfect but reliable response to its acts = another agent, no
   response = background (Watson; Johnson, Slaughter & Carey 1998) - the same logic as self marker 1 and as S3b, turned outward;
3. predicting another mover with its own going machinery under an efficiency assumption (does that mover end where my food and water are?) -
   the link to its insides is a hypothesis, not a finding.
Cheap diagnostic before any part (theory first): does the being's own frame forecast already err differently on the drawn other than on
walls and springs, and differently when the other responds to it than when it does not? If yes, (2) can grow from what it has; if no, that is
the missing bias - a bedrock decision for the founder.

## The furthest step: from a model of the world to a model of itself (founder 2026-11-25: "not Jarvis - Vision")
Jarvis is a set of functions; Vision is one being with a history. The map above is about the world and others; the hardest step is the being
modelling itself - what it learned, what it dropped, what it is becoming - kept without text or symbols.
- Now: its changes are recorded only by us (S3b's switch log, value and memory states). The being does not sense its own changes.
- Minimal non-symbolic form (to be registered later, one step at a time): (1) its own inner changes become something it senses - which memory
  context it is in, how surprised it has been, how its choice in a familiar situation has shifted (interoception of its own mind); (2) it
  forecasts its own next choice from that sense, as it forecasts the world (a self-model is the being predicting itself; cf. Metzinger's
  self-model theory, Graziano's attention schema - theories, not results); (3) a measure: in a situation met before, does it act differently
  after a change it went through, and does its own forecast of itself follow (yesterday's self vs today's).
- Order: agent detection (Tier 1) and this self-model are two halves of one thing - "that mover predicts and acts like I do" needs both. Which
  comes first in development is debated (simulation theory: self first; Heyes / social-construction views: others first); ATANOR measures,
  does not assume.

# D38 (registered before running): does its own forecaster tell a thing that reacts to it from one that moves on its own?
Founder 2026-11-25: register the agent-detection diagnostic. No new part (tools/d38_agent.py). The S3-end being lives in room 9101 / 9104,
64 bodies in thirds, each body with one other of its kind drawn in its eye: still (stands), responsive (each beat moves by .7 of its body's
last displacement + small noise: it reacts to what the body does), yoked (replays a responsive other's moves of another body: same motion, not
caused by this body). Measure: its own forecaster's error (Core.foresee, the chosen gesture) on the eye part of the sense when the other is
seen, minus the same body group's error when unseen ("excess"), first vs last 1,000 of 5,000 beats; the mind lives and learns as always.
Found while reading for it: the eye already has an innate "bug" bias (Organs._bug: a small mover at the centre changing more than its
surround, frog-retina style - own turning cancels it), and Mind has a learning-progress reward option (lp, off by default).
Smoke (2,000 beats, 9101, not a result): excess responsive .011 -> .008, yoked .012 -> .012, still .013 -> .015; seen shares 12% / 8% / 2-4%.
Bars: in both layouts, responsive excess in the last 1,000 beats at most 80% of yoked, and responsive excess falls first -> last by more than
yoked's. Readings: met -> its own act-conditioned forecaster learns the other that answers it better than an equal mover that does not: the
raw seed of "that reacts to what I do" is there; next is making that difference something the being itself senses (a contingency sense,
then the self/other sort). Not met -> the seed is missing at this grain (the other is small in the eye, or the forecaster ignores it); then
an attention or contingency bias is the founder's decision.

## D38 result (2 layouts, 5,000 beats): not met as registered - the direction holds in both, the size in one
Excess eye-forecast error when the other is seen (first 1,000 -> last 1,000 beats):
| layout | still | responsive | yoked | responsive / yoked (last) | fall responsive vs yoked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9101 | .0135 -> .0120 | .0111 -> .0091 | .0117 -> .0127 | .71 (bar <= .80 met) | .0020 vs -.0010 (met) |
| 9104 | .0129 -> .0098 | .0113 -> .0082 | .0124 -> .0097 | .85 (not met) | .0031 vs .0028 (barely) |
Seen shares: still 2-5%, responsive 12-13%, yoked 8-9%. Reading: in both rooms the being's own act-conditioned forecaster does better on
the other that answers its acts than on an equal mover that does not, from the start (.011 vs .012) and at the end; in 9101 only it learns
the answering one while not the other. A real but small seed - the forecaster carries some "that moves with what I do", not enough to stand
as a sorting the being could use. Not a pass. Caveat: one start per layout; the responsive other also stays in view more (12% vs 8%), which
alone could ease its forecast. Next (registered before running): the contingency made something the being itself senses - its own error on
the seen other, split by whether it acted - before any new bias; if that sense stays flat, a contingency bias is the founder's decision.

# D38b (registered before running; the follow-up D38 named, after S4h showed the young cannot follow what it does not hold as one other)
Same run as D38 (tools/d38_agent.py, S3-end being, 9101 and 9104, 5,000 beats, one start), its own forecast error on the eye when the other is
seen, now split by whether its own act moved it that beat (moved > .05 cell) or not. The contingency the being could sense: when it stands,
the answering other stands too (only noise), the yoked one goes on moving. Measure, last 1,000 beats: C_still = yoked seen-still error minus
responsive seen-still error; C_moved = the same on moving beats.
Reading (diagnostic, no bar beyond this): C_still > 0 and at least 10% of the responsive seen-still error in both layouts, and larger than
C_moved -> its own errors already carry "that one stops when I stop": a signal a contingency sense could read without a new bias. Flat ->
a contingency bias is the founder's decision.

## D38b result: not met - the sign differs by room
Last 1,000 beats, own eye-forecast error on the seen other, beats it stood still / moved:
| layout | responsive still / moved | yoked still / moved | C_still (rel.) | C_moved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9101 | .0191 / .0179 | .0240 / .0213 | +.0049 (+26%) | +.0034 |
| 9104 | .0227 / .0173 | .0187 / .0192 | -.0040 (-18%) | +.0019 |
Met in 9101, reversed in 9104 -> not met in both. Caveat: standing-still beats are 7-13% of seen beats (a few hundred samples per group),
so the split is noisy; but nothing here says the being's own errors carry "that one stops when I stop" reliably. As registered: a contingency
bias (a bedrock-level pull to notice what moves with its own acts - infants' contingency detection, Watson 1972; Gergely & Watson 1999) is the
founder's decision. Held until then; no bias is added.

# D38c (registered before running; no new part, a sharper look than D38b, which had few standing beats)
Same run. Each beat: how much its own eye forecast differs across the acts it could make (variance over gestures of Core.foresee on the eye
part), on beats the other is seen minus unseen ("act excess"), per group, first vs last 1,000 of 5,000 beats. If the forecaster has learned
"that one moves with what I do", the act excess is larger for the responsive other than for the yoked one, and grows.
Reading (diagnostic): responsive act excess last >= 1.2x yoked in both layouts, and grown from first -> the seed of contingency is in its own
forecaster, readable as a sense without a bias; else not, and the contingency bias stays the founder's decision.

## D38c result: not met - no seed of contingency in its own forecaster
Act excess (variance of its eye forecast over its acts, seen minus unseen), first -> last 1,000 beats:
| layout | still | responsive | yoked | responsive / yoked (last) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9101 | .0020 -> -.0002 | .0042 -> .0045 | .0051 -> .0056 | .80 |
| 9104 | .0031 -> .0007 | .0059 -> .0057 | .0067 -> .0058 | .98 |
The forecast hangs on its own act no more for the other that answers it than for one that does not, and it does not grow. With D38 (small,
one room) and D38b (sign differs by room), three looks agree: in 5,000 beats the being's own learning does not pick out "that one moves with
what I do". The social line (S4g-S4h: no following; the guardian only feeds) stops here at the same wall: there is no "other" in it to follow.
As registered: whether to give a contingency bedrock (infants' contingency detection, Watson 1972; Gergely & Watson 1999) is the founder's
decision. Nothing is added until then.
