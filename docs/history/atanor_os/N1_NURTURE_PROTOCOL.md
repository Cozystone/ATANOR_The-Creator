## The human way, as the reference
- In arms first. An infant is carried through its family's range and fed before it can go anywhere. It sees the range long before it walks it.
- Being carried is not enough for skill. Held & Hein (1963): kittens moved passively through the same sights as self-moving litter-mates did not develop
  sight-guided movement. So the carried stage can give the map, not the going.
- Then on its own legs, near the caregiver. Bowlby (1969), Ainsworth: the caregiver is a secure base; the child walks after it and comes back to it.
  Harlow (1958): this bond is not derived from feeding in monkeys - it is evolved. (That matters for the choice below.)
- Help is taken away as the child becomes able. Wood, Bruner & Ross (1976), scaffolding; Vygotsky's zone of proximal development; in other animals
  Thornton & McAuliffe (2006): meerkat helpers grade their help by the pup's age. Weaning: the parent stops feeding and the young must reach the food.
- Later: watching others of its kind succeed or die (the founder's community stage). Not in N1.

## The childhood (all of it is the world's doing; nothing is added to the mind's choosing)
One caregiver body per young body, driven by the judge (it goes to the spring its own insides need; it is part of the world, as a parent is).
The young sees it with its own eye (the room must draw it: a body-high shape of its own colour) and nothing else is told to the young.
1. In arms (first eighth of the life): the young is carried at the caregiver's place and heading; its own gestures move nothing; when the caregiver
   drinks or eats, the young's same inside rises too (it is fed). Its senses, place memory and value learn as always.
2. Walking after (to the half of the life): the young moves by its own gestures. The caregiver walks its trips a little ahead and waits when the young is
   more than a few cells behind. Feeding by the caregiver thins out over this stage to nothing (weaning): at the end the young only fills at a spring itself.
3. Leaving (to three quarters): the caregiver is present on fewer and fewer beats; then it is gone.
4. Alone (last quarter): the measured part.

(ii) If (i) does not make it follow: nearness to its own kind as an evolved bedrock (Harlow's result says this is the human way). That is a new fixed
