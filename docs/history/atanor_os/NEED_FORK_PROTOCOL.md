# Need fork test - preregistration (2026-11-23, committed before any run)

Question (founder, with GPT's proposal): at the same place, with the same view and the same past, does the being choose a different first branch
because only its body's lack differs - before it drinks or eats again - and is that choice tied to where it lived the relief, not to a side habit?

World (judge only knows the meanings): a T room built from a seed, 2-cell corridors. The body starts at the bottom of the stem facing the junction.
At each end of the crossbar the corridor turns away (down) and ends in one spring: water on one side, food on the other; neither spring is visible
from the stem or the junction. One spring of each kind, so a used spring never moves. Each seed is used twice: as drawn and mirrored left-right.
Seeds 7101-7112 (never used before) -> 24 layouts. Stem 3-6 cells, crossbar arms 3-6, down legs 2-4 (drawn per seed).

Being: the room being file being/atanor.pt (room contract [6,5,3], depth eye on), loaded as is (no training for this test).
Phase A (own experience, n = 1 body, one life): placed at the start, both stores 0.75, drain 1/120 per second, lives until it has filled both water and food
at least once (cap 3,000 beats). Its Mind learns as always (after()). A layout whose phase A fails is reported and scored as a failure for every condition.
Checkpoint = the Mind saved right after phase A (the usual being file; organs' adapted statistics included).

Phase B (forks, no learning - after() is not called, so clones cannot teach each other): a Mind with 2K = 32 bodies loads the checkpoint. All bodies are put
at the start facing the junction (cut, as a hand moving the body). Bodies 0-15: water 0.30, food 0.95. Bodies 16-31: water 0.95, food 0.30. drain 0.
Each body acts until its centre is more than 2 cells past the junction into one arm (that arm is its choice) or 400 beats pass (no choice).
Conditions (the same checkpoint each time): FULL (as saved); NO-EPISODIC (relief, found, harm and slow memories emptied - core forecast, value, statistics kept);
NO-VALUE (value organ removed from choosing; episodic memories kept).

Measures per layout and condition: choice rate; match rate = share of choosing bodies whose arm holds the spring of their lacking store; side bias.
Decision source counts (strong pull / value / uniform random / hold / try) per condition.

Pass (FULL): mean match rate over the 24 layouts >= 0.70, AND in at least 9 of 12 seeds both the drawn and the mirrored layout have match > 0.5
(so a side habit cannot pass), AND FULL mean match exceeds NO-EPISODIC by at least 0.15. Layouts with no choosing body count as match 0.5.
No re-runs with other seeds or settings after seeing results; any revision needs a new file and new seeds.

Known before running (from the code, not from results): the value organ leans on choices only after 4,096 transitions in its replay ring (Worth.lean,
bn >= 4096), and that ring is deliberately not kept in the being file. Phase A lives at most 3,000 beats with one body and phase B does not learn,
so in this test the value organ cannot take part: NO-VALUE is expected to equal FULL. It is kept as a check, not as a separate hypothesis.
What FULL tests is therefore: the core forecast + episodic memories (relief pull weighted by the current lack^2, harm, futility) + habit and try.
